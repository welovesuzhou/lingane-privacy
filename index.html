What is 灵感鹅?

灵感鹅 is a workflow tool designed for image creative operators. It lets you upload a reference image, use visual AI to reverse-engineer prompts, edit and refine them, generate images, and deliver results to Figma or Feishu — all in a streamlined pipeline.

Core Features

• Right-click any image in Chrome to instantly generate AI prompts
• Streaming vision AI analysis (Auto & Smart modes)
• Text-to-image and image-to-image generation
• One-click export to Figma tables and Feishu multi-dimensional tables
• Quota management with free and paid balance tiers
