Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

灵感鹅 ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the 灵感鹅 Chrome Extension and web application.

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you register.
  • Images you upload: Images are sent to our AI vision service to generate prompts. Images may be stored temporarily on our servers to provide persistent URLs for your workflow.
  • Usage data: Number of API calls made (for quota tracking), timestamps of usage. We do not collect browsing history or data from pages you visit.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the 灵感鹅 service
  • To manage your account and usage quotas
  • To process payments for paid plans (via third-party payment providers)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track your browsing history or activity on websites you visit
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not use your images for AI model training
  • The extension only activates when you explicitly trigger a right-click action

Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely on servers located in China (Alibaba Cloud). We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Authentication uses industry-standard JWT tokens with short expiry periods.

Third-Party Services

灵感鹅 integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Third-party AI image and model service providers: For vision analysis and image generation (your images may be processed by their APIs)
  • Figma / Feishu (Lark): Only when you explicitly choose to export results
  • Payment providers (WeChat Pay / Alipay): For processing paid plan purchases

Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the support page.
