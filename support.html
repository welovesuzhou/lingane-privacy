Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the Chrome extension?

After installing the extension, right-click on any image on a webpage and select the 灵感鹅 option to instantly analyze the image and generate AI prompts. Make sure you are logged in to your 灵感鹅 account first.

Why can't I see the login overlay in the extension?

The extension requires the 灵感鹅 service to be running. If you are using a self-hosted instance, ensure your API server is online. If you are using the hosted service, please check your internet connection.

What is the difference between Auto and Smart mode?

Auto mode uses the standard vision AI at a higher temperature for more creative and varied prompt output. Smart mode uses a lower temperature for more precise and structured prompts. Smart mode consumes Smart quota; see the quota page in your account for details.

My free quota ran out. How do I continue?

You can purchase a recharge pack through the extension's side panel or via the web application. Open the side panel, click "Recharge" and follow the prompts to complete payment via WeChat Pay or Alipay.

How do I update the extension?

When a new version is available, a banner will appear in the extension's side panel. Click "Download Update" to download the new version, then replace your extension folder and reload it in chrome://extensions.

How do I export to Figma?

In Step 3 of the workflow, configure your Figma URL and channel in Settings, then use the "Export to Figma" button to send your prompts and generated images directly to your Figma table.
